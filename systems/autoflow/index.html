AutoFlow

Describe your business in a 6-step form; get a deployed n8n workflow and an emailed report.

my role Built it solo: 41 commits in 5 days.

The problem

Small businesses know they should automate but not where to start, and turning an idea into a working workflow usually takes a consultant and days of back and forth.

How it works

  1. A 6-step form captures the industry, team, tools and the tasks that eat time.
  2. Claude ranks the top three automation opportunities with a few-shot prompt at temperature 0.
  3. It writes an n8n workflow for the best one, built from tested templates.
  4. The n8n API creates and turns on the workflow.
  5. Resend emails an HTML report, and the results page shows the analysis in charts.

What changed

  • 6form steps
  • 9workflow templates
  • 120 stime limit for the whole run
  • 41commits in 5 days

What I built
