Soch SEO Blogs App

One keyword in, a researched and illustrated blog post out, for any of six websites.

my role Designed and built it: 89 of 110 commits. Rebuilt from a 152-node n8n workflow.

The problem

Soch publishes blogs on six websites through a 152-node n8n workflow with three approval gates. I rebuilt it as an app so it can be tested, watched and changed safely, with a clear cost for every post.

How it works

  1. Pick a site and a keyword. The worker researches it with DataForSEO and by reading the top results.
  2. Claude writes ideas, then a brief, then the article, using that site's own prompts, model and budget.
  3. Internal and external links are added, then images: Claude vision scores stock photos and gpt-image-2 fills any gaps.
  4. A person reviews the draft in the studio, and posts can be translated.
  5. Publishing is a commit to the site's GitHub repo, which deploys the site.
  6. An autopilot drafts about two posts a week per site inside a weekly budget, and retries never pay for the same step twice.

What changed

What I built

  • A cost ledger that records every paid call, and the weekly budget cap.
  • 723 automated tests across 66 test files.
