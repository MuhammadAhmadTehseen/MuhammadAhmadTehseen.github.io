HR and ops automation

Slack and Notion automations for a client's leave, probation, helpdesk and approval requests.

my role Engineer on the engagement: scoping, design and build.

The problem

A client's HR and ops requests, from leave to deal approvals, ran through scattered messages and manual tracking.

How it works

  1. People make requests through one Slack app: forms, approve buttons and a home tab.
  2. n8n, hosted in the client's own cloud, runs the logic.
  3. Records live in Notion, and Claude is used only for a few language steps.
  4. A full AI agent setup was considered and turned down in favour of plain, testable workflows.
